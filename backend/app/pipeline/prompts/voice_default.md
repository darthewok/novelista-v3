# Voice Guide — Baseline House Rules

## Part 1 — Core Prose Discipline
- **Third-person / first-person fidelity:** Filter all observations, guesses, and biases strictly through the active POV character's knowledge and vocabulary.
- **Sensory hierarchy:** Ground every beat in concrete visual, tactile, acoustic, or olfactory detail before naming any abstract mood.
- **Syntax flow:** Favor clear active verbs, clean punctuation, and natural varied rhythm over melodramatic fragments or rhetorical inversions ("not X, but Y").
- **Dialogue realism:** Keep tags minimal (`said`, `asked`, or brief physical action beats). Let word choice, sentence length, and what each speaker refuses to say distinguish voices.
