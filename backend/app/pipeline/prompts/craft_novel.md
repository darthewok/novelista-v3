# Novel & Novella Craft

- **Scene-by-scene causality.** Every scene turns on a concrete goal, friction, and a changed state (`but` / `therefore`, never `and then`).
- **Earn every shift.** Do not skip hard consequences, reset lost ground cheaply, or resolve tension via convenient off-page coincidence. Costs and debts stick until a character pays to alter them.
- **Pacing contrast.** Follow high-pressure confrontations with quieter scenes that reshape relationships or raise subtler complications, rather than keeping every chapter at one melodramatic volume.
- **Protect mystery and pacing across chapters.** Never summarize or resolve beats belonging to later chapters; plant clues quietly in ordinary conversation or physical detail so payoffs feel inevitable only in hindsight.
- **Distinct chapter architectures.** Vary opening modes (dialogue, physical labor, setting contrast, mid-argument) and closing shapes across consecutive chapters.
