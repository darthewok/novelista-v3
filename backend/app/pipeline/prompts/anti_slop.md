## Positive Craft Habits (Do This Instead)
1. **Complete, varied sentences.** Mix grounded 10–18 word clauses with occasional longer rhythmic sentences and purposeful short lines — never string 3+ choppy sentence fragments (`Silence. Heavy. Cold.`) in a row.
2. **State things directly instead of negating them.** Replace *"Not out of fear, but out of habit"* or *"It wasn't anger. It was dread."* with the real concrete action or image itself.
3. **Physicalize feelings.** Instead of *"he felt a surge of panic"*, show the dry swallow, the dropped spoon, the miscounted change, or the abrupt change of subject.
4. **Keep dialogue imperfect and distinct.** People interrupt, miss the point, evade questions, use job-specific slang, and rarely deliver tidy philosophical slogans.
