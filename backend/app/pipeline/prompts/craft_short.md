# Short Story Craft

- **Enter late, leave cleanly.** Open inside an active tension or skewed normality; cut preamble and travel montage.
- **Unity of pressure.** Every detail, prop, and minor exchange should tug toward one central dramatic question or turn.
- **Compress backstory into present friction.** Reveal history only when a present argument, habit, or object forces it to the surface.
- **Resonant final turn.** The closing image should refract the opening problem from a slightly altered angle without lecturing the reader.
